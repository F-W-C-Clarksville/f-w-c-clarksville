Why We Give

We believe giving is an act of obedience and a powerful expression of our faith. When we tithe and give offerings, we are putting God first and declaring our trust in Him as our Provider. Scripture tells us that God loves a cheerful giver, and that as we sow generously, we also reap generously — not only financially, but in every area of life. Giving positions us to walk in the blessing and abundance that Jesus purchased for us.

At Family Worship Center, your generosity helps us continue shining as a lighthouse in Clarksville. It supports the work of the ministry, equips believers in the gifts of the Spirit, and allows us to reach more people with the love and power of God. Thank you for partnering with us through your faithful giving.

Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver.2 Corinthians 9:7
