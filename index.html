Welcome Home

We are a warm, family-oriented church that loves Jesus and loves people. Our vision is simple: to be a lighthouse in Clarksville — shining the hope of the Gospel into our community and beyond.

We believe the gifts of the Holy Spirit are still active and vital today. God continues to heal, strengthen, and equip His people through the Spirit’s power so that we can walk in freedom and impact the world around us.
