What to Expect

We want your first visit to feel comfortable and meaningful.

Family Friendly

A warm atmosphere for all ages. Children are a blessing and welcome in our services.

Spirit-Empowered

We believe the gifts of the Spirit are for today — including healing, prophetic ministry, and power for everyday life.

Casual & Welcoming

Come as you are. There’s no dress code — just a desire to encounter Jesus together.

Genuine Community

Real people growing in faith, hope, and friendship. You’ll be greeted with kindness.

Kids & Youth

We love seeing families grow together in faith. Children’s ministry and youth opportunities are part of life at Family Worship Center. Feel free to bring your kids — they are always welcome.

First-Time Visitor FAQ

What should I wear?

Come as you are. Most people dress casually. The focus is on Jesus, not clothing.

Where do I park?

We share the parking lot with Dos Margaritas Mexican Restaurant at 1273 Northfield Dr. Plenty of parking is available right in front of the building.

Is it okay to bring kids?

Absolutely. Children are a blessing here and are welcome in our services.

How long is the service?

Sunday morning worship typically lasts about 90 minutes. Wednesday evenings are focused teaching and ministry times.

What if I’m new to church?

You are completely welcome. We are a family-oriented, Bible-believing church that loves people right where they are.
