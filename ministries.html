Ministries

From Family Worship Center, streams of ministry continue to flow — equipping believers, training in the gifts of the Spirit, and reaching our community with the love of Jesus.

Core Ministries

We desire every generation to grow strong in faith and in the power of the Holy Spirit.

Children’s Ministry

We love seeing kids encounter Jesus. Children’s Church, nursery care, and special times such as AWANAs and VBS help the next generation build a strong foundation of faith.

Youth Ministry

Our youth are encouraged to grow in relationship with God, walk in the gifts of the Spirit, and discover their calling in a supportive, faith-filled environment.

Adult & Family Ministry

Through worship, teaching, and fellowship, we help families and adults grow deeper in the Word and in the power of the Holy Spirit for everyday life.

Ministries Flowing Out

Family Worship Center is a launching place. Other ministries continue to flow from this house — equipping believers in the gifts of the Spirit, training in healing and prophetic ministry, and impacting the region.

Gary & Ellen McGillvary

Gifts of Healings

Every Wednesday at 6:30 PM, Gary McGillvary leads focused training on the Gifts of Healings. Believers are equipped to walk in the healing power of the Holy Spirit for today — bringing freedom, restoration, and the tangible presence of God into everyday life.

Ellen serves alongside Gary in this ministry, welcoming people into the warmth of this house and the presence of God.

Doug & Yvette Kent

Transformation Worship Center

We are blessed to partner with Doug and Yvette Kent of Transformation Worship Center. Their ministry focuses on activating the gifts of the Holy Spirit, prophetic training, and equipping the five-fold ministry so that believers can walk in power and purpose.

How to Get Involved

Whether you want to serve, grow in the gifts of the Spirit, or simply find your place in the family, we would love to walk with you.

Come to a service, speak with one of our leaders, or reach out — we are here to help you take the next step.
